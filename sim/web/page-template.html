Things to try

  • Spin the green Oxygen wheel to 32, then start the compressor and watch the mix settle.
  • Tap Presets and pick a trimix like 18/45.
  • Hit EMERGENCY STOP, then tap it again to resume.
  • Setup → Blender Setup: switch to Nitrox only, or BAR.
  • Run Calibrate, and try Setup → Pressure Calibration.
